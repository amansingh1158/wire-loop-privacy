Privacy Policy — Wire Loop

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Android game, package com.wireloop.puzzle

This policy explains what information the mobile game Wire Loop (the “Game”) handles. The Game is published on Google Play under the package name com.wireloop.puzzle.

The short version: the Game has no server, no account of its own, and does not collect or transmit your gameplay data to us. We cannot see how you play and we never receive your progress. The only information that leaves your device comes from the advertising and sign-in services described below, and those are operated by Google.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • your contacts, photos, files, calendar, or any other personal files
  • your precise or approximate location
  • your gameplay progress, scores, or statistics
  • any account credentials

The Game has no backend server. Nothing you do in the Game is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

2. Information stored on your device

Wire Loop saves your progress locally on your device only, using your device’s standard app storage. This includes your current level and best result, your coin balance, and which shop upgrades you have purchased and their levels.

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to us and we cannot read it. If you uninstall the Game, or clear its data in Android settings, this information is deleted. We cannot restore it, because we never had it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob, including a banner ad and optional rewarded video ads. The Game contains ads, and this is declared on its Google Play listing.

To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • device type, operating system version, and general device information
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • how you interact with an ad, such as whether it was viewed or clicked

This data is collected by Google as an independent controller, not by us. We do not receive your advertising ID and we cannot identify you from it. We only receive aggregate, anonymous reports, such as total ad impressions and revenue, which contain no information about individual players.

Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s privacy policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising, and reset or delete your advertising ID, in your Android device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

The Game offers optional sign-in with Google Play Games Services. You can play the entire Game without ever signing in, and declining sign-in does not limit any feature.

If you choose to sign in, the Game receives from Google Play Games your Play Games display name and avatar image URL. This is used only to show your name and avatar in the Game’s interface. It is held in memory while the Game is running and is not stored by us or transmitted anywhere. The Game does not use leaderboards, achievements, or any other Play Games feature.

Sign-in is handled entirely by Google. We never receive your Google account credentials or email address. Google’s handling of Play Games data is governed by Google’s privacy policy linked in section 3.

You can revoke the Game’s access at any time, and manage or disconnect Play Games, through your Google account permissions.

5. Children’s privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the Game does not collect personal information itself, there is nothing for us to delete on request. Advertising is served by Google AdMob and is subject to Google’s own policies regarding children’s data.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us using the address below and we will assist.

6. Data security and retention

We hold no personal data about you, so there is no personal data for us to secure, retain, or breach. Progress data lives only on your device and is retained there until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Advertising and sign-in data are retained by Google according to Google’s policies.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, we cannot provide, correct, export, or delete data on your behalf — we simply do not have it.

For data held by Google through advertising or Play Games, you can exercise your rights directly with Google using the links in sections 3 and 4. If you are in the EEA, the UK, California, or another region with data protection laws, those rights apply to the processing performed by Google as described above.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the Game changes, for example if new features or services are added. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
